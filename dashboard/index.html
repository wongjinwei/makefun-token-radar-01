Method — model radar-2

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains. The category multiple is the median mcap/TVL among live-token protocols in the same category, used only when at least 3 live protocols back it and the median is positive; thinner, degenerate or empty categories fall back to the global median. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −60%…+120%, weighted 0.5: growth = 1 + clamp/100 × 0.5 (no reported change counts as 0). Maturity is 0.6 + min(age, 1095)/1095 × 0.4 (unknown age counts as 365 days). Chains multiply by 1 + 0.02 × min(chains, 5). The result is rounded to whole dollars, at least $1. Only the snapshot's own figures are used; no list of names is hardcoded. A protocol "records no token" when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol — that is the record's word, not proof it has none.
